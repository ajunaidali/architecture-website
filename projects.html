Featured Project

Mosque Dome Construction

A premium showcase of our completed domed mosque — engineered from foundation to crescent with timeless craftsmanship.

Completed

Domed Mosque

A domed mosque is a shell-structure masterpiece characterized by a large curved roof that efficiently resists compression, clear-span prayer halls, and complex load paths requiring rigorous analysis of ring beams and lateral forces.

Project TypeMosque Dome Construction
Dome StyleRCC Islamic Dome
Construction StatusCompleted
FinishingPremium Exterior Paint
LocationShah Waliyat Madarsa, Maymar
Step By Step

Construction Journey

Tap any image to explore the build in our fullscreen gallery.

Step 01

Foundation & Base Structure

A reinforced concrete foundation and base structure were constructed to provide maximum stability and ensure long-term structural performance for the mosque dome.

Step 02

RCC Dome Construction

The reinforced cement concrete (RCC) dome framework was carefully engineered and cast to achieve exceptional structural strength and architectural precision.

Step 03

Concrete Finishing

Concrete surfaces were refined and leveled to eliminate imperfections, creating a durable and uniform base for the finishing process.

Step 04

Surface Preparation

The entire dome surface was cleaned, repaired, and prepared to ensure excellent adhesion for protective coatings and decorative finishes.

Step 05

Primer & Waterproofing

Premium waterproofing systems and high-performance primers were applied to protect the dome from moisture, weather exposure, and long-term deterioration.

Step 06

Final Paint Finish

Multiple coats of premium exterior architectural paint were applied to deliver a refined appearance while enhancing durability and UV resistance.

Step 07

Crescent Installation

The decorative crescent was securely installed at the apex of the dome, completing its iconic architectural identity.

Step 08

Completed Mosque Dome

The completed mosque dome showcases superior craftsmanship, elegant proportions, and a timeless architectural presence, ready to serve the community.
